## Description

Sleep in beds outside your farmhouse using the game's normal sleep prompt.

## Installation instructions

1. Install SMAPI.
2. Extract `SleepOnAnyBed` into your game's `Mods` folder.
3. Launch through SMAPI and approach a bed to open the sleep prompt.

## Main features

- Enables the normal sleep prompt outside the farmhouse.
- Recognizes bed furniture and NPC home sleeping spots.
- Leaves the farmhouse and island farmhouse's native sleep handling in place.

## Requirements

- SMAPI 4.5.2+.
